The FDO Forum deeply regrets to share that our Steering Committee member, Jonathan Clark, died suddenly on August 11, 2026. 
Almost from the beginning of the FDO Forum in 2021, Jonathan was an active member bringing in many excellent contributions based on his broad experience - from  the domain of electronic publishing and beyond, that helped the Forum to define actions and strategies. We also lost a colleague whose commitment and kindness created a positive and productive atmosphere. 
We enjoyed working with him on a personal level and loved hearing about his many interwoven passions - living in the sunny Croatian mountains, his gustatory pursuits, cycling, and his advocacy in the lymphoma community. 
We will remember Jonathan fondly and send his family our warmest thoughts and sincere condolences.
