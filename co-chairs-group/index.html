Executive Committee

The members of the Executive Committee (EC) are appointed by the Steering Committee (SC) and have the following tasks:

  • prepare and chair the SC and Advisory Committee (AC) meetings and create the reports,
  • stimulate FDOF discussions and processes based on deep insights and engagement,
  • turn SC decisions into actions and bring in AC’s advice,
  • take care of the secretarial tasks (website, email lists, etc.),
  • check whether the tasks can be managed given the voluntary basis and restricted funding of FDOF,
  • makes tactical or short fuse decisions related to carrying out the general direction from the SC,
  • draft the FDO Bulletin as a general and regular note to the FDO community.
