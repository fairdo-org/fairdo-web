## What are FAIR Data Principles?

FAIR stands for **Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable**. These principles were first published in 2016 and have since become the gold standard for scientific data management.

The goal of FAIR is to make data more useful — not just for humans, but also for machines. In an era of big data and automated analysis, having well-structured, well-described data is more important than ever.
