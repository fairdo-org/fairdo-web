FDO Specifications 2026

🕒 14:30 CEST 📍 Online
Speakers: Sven Bingert, Jana Böhm, Maximilian Inckmann; During the last year, the FDO specifications underwent a major reorganization: from a collection of official notes to one consolidated and harmonized W3C-style specification document. These updated specifications are built on five years of practical experience in creating, managing, and utilizing FDOs across various projects.
