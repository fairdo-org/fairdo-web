As part of the 2022 conference in Leiden the FDOF developed participation guidelines which we see as a [Code of Conduct](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1bsK4gn1132zwzzTl79btr0WwE62lsOHAA9oMEpZt0XA/edit?usp=sharing) guiding all activities of the FDOF. We expect any FDOF member to act according to these guidelines.
