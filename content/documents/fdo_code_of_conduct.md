As part of the 2022 conference in Leiden the FDOF developed participation guidelines which we see as a [Code of Conduct](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21872745) ([10.5281/zenodo.21872745](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21872745)) guiding all activities of the FDOF. We expect any FDOF member to act according to these guidelines.
