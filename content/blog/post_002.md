## The State of Open Science Today

Open science is no longer a fringe movement. Across the globe, funding agencies, universities, and research institutions are mandating open access to publications and data. But what does open science look like in practice?

We spoke with researchers from three different fields to find out how they are navigating the transition to more open ways of working.
